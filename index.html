2026Web app

SettleUp

A Splitwise-style app for splitting bills with friends. Groups for trips and homes, friends who haven't signed up yet, expenses split equally, by exact amounts, percentages or shares with one or several payers, and a "How we simplified" page that walks through every step of settling a group's debts.

Built with
Python, Django, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS; one-click deploy to Render from a blueprint
Design
Every split type reduces to paid and owed per person; all money math in integer cents; a service layer owns every permission check and write
Algorithm
Debt simplification nets each person's balance and settles a group of n people in at most n − 1 payments, kept separately per currency
Tested with
16 Django tests, including randomized groups that check nets are preserved and the payment bound holds, splits that always sum to the total, and outsiders blocked from groups
